About Us

Welcome to Happy Planting Co.

We believe that you do not need a massive backyard or a perfect green thumb to create your own personal oasis. Our journey started in a cramped apartment with three half-dead succulents and a lot of determination. Today, we are a thriving community of everyday growers, patio farmers, and windowsill horticulturists.

This website is built for the absolute beginner and the weekend warrior alike. We break down complex gardening concepts into simple, budget-friendly, step-by-step projects.

Grab your trowel, embrace the mud, and let us grow something beautiful together.

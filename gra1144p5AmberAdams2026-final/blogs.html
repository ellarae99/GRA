The Quiet of the Green Mountains

There is a specific kind of silence you only find when the snow starts to melt but the leaves haven't quite figured out they're allowed to grow yet. We spent the last three days in a cabin with no cell service, just tracking the demos for The Great Divide.

Most of the songs were written on an old upright piano that was out of tune, but it felt right for the mood we were trying to capture. This journey has been about finding beauty in the gaps between the seasons.

"The best songs aren't written; they're just overheard in the woods."

We're heading back to the city tomorrow to start the mixing process. It's going to be a long drive, but I've got a trunk full of tapes and a head full of ideas.

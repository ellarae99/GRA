Privacy Policy: The "Trust Us, We’re Digital" Promise

Effective Date: April 1, 2026
Last Updated: Roughly five minutes before you clicked this.

Welcome to our Privacy Statement. We know you aren't actually reading this, but our legal team says we have to put these words in this specific order to keep the lights on. Here is how we handle your data with the care of a caffeinated squirrel.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily give us, and some that we just sort of... find.

  • Personal Identifiers: Your name, email address, and that one embarrassing username you created in 2009 and still use for everything.
  • Digital Breadcrumbs: Your IP address, browser type, and how many times you hovered your mouse over the "Buy Now" button before having a brief existential crisis.
  • The "Vibe" Check: Our algorithms may attempt to guess your mood based on how aggressively you scroll through our terms of service.

How We Use Your Information

We use your data to make your experience "seamless," which is tech-speak for "we want to know what you're thinking before you do."

  • To Annoy You (Marketing): Sending you "personalized" emails at 3:00 AM about products you looked at once while sleep-deprived.
  • To Improve Our Site: Fixing the bugs that we definitely didn't put there on purpose.
  • To Fuel the Machine: Training our AI to understand human sarcasm (it’s currently failing).

Sharing Your Data

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do, however, "lease" it to "strategic partners" who happen to have very large databases and a keen interest in your shopping habits.

Your Choices (The Illusion of Control)

You have rights! Sort of.

  • Opt-Out: You can click the "Unsubscribe" link in our emails. It may take 10 business days to process, during which time we will send you seven "We Miss You" emails.
  • Cookies: You can disable cookies in your browser, but be warned: our website might look like a Geocities page from 1998 if you do.

Security

We protect your data using industry-standard encryption, three firewalls, and a very stern "Do Not Enter" sign on our server room door. While we strive to protect your info, please remember that the internet is essentially a giant glass house in a hailstone factory.
